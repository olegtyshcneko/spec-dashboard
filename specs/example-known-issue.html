A known-issue spec documents a bug or limitation that's been seen and accepted (at least for now). The status makes it visible — pretending an issue doesn't exist tends to surface as repeat support tickets months later. Promote to wip when someone starts fixing it.

Why it hasn't been fixed yet

Be honest. Some issues are intentionally deferred (rare edge case, low-impact, blocked on dependency). Some just haven't been prioritized. Both are fine reasons — just record which it is.
