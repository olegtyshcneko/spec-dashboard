Nice to have

Example: nice-to-have idea

Nice-to-haves are ideas the team thinks would be cool but isn't planning to ship soon. The point is to capture the idea so it's not lost, while making clear it's not blocking anything else. If a nice-to-have ever feels load-bearing, promote it to backlog.

Idea

Describe the thing you wish existed. Skip the full user-story / acceptance-criteria machinery if it's not warranted yet — nice-to-haves are allowed to be sketches.

Why we haven't picked this up

  • Lower ROI than current backlog items
  • Depends on something not yet built
  • Whatever the actual reason is — record it so the next person doesn't have to rediscover it
